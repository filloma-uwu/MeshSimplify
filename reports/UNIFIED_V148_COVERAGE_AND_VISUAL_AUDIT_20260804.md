## Final conservative-coverage gate

The exporter now preserves the selected convex-volume certificates across
containment removal and coplanar union. Before writing the final OBJ, every
non-degenerate source triangle must satisfy both conditions:

1. it has explicit responsibility in the final primitive set; and
2. it is either inside a selected convex enclosure or exactly covered by the
   union of coplanar final surface primitives.

Any unassigned or uncovered source triangle aborts export and writes
`coverage_failed_faces.obj` plus `coverage_failure.txt` for diagnosis.
